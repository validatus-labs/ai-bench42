# A fresh coding attempt with declared recipe changes

A new independent attempt used recommended sampling, explicit High reasoning and
a smaller common handoff. The empty assignment, tests, approved development data
and numerical budgets were preserved. Own browser proof and independent readiness
preceded coding. This was a diagnostic cohort, not a sampling-only causal test.

The model ended naturally after 25 minutes 30.56 seconds: 47 coding requests,
44,888 generated tokens and 113 tools. All 21 original and seven worker tests
passed. The common independent probes passed 17 of 27; MIME, source fidelity
and safe output defects remained. Full development failed.

All 49 whole-trial requests, including readiness, completed successfully. No
context, time or tool-budget exhaustion caused the natural stop, and the earlier
exact read cycle was not detected. A blocked on-demand search-tool download was
an observed toolchain gap; other tools remained usable. Research/reporting were
also incomplete. Independent review preserved the failed candidate and verified
reuse without inference. Other recipe/handoff differences prevent assigning the
change in probe results to sampling alone.
